02 · Clarify: When and Where?

12 minutes · Read the conversation, then explore the language.

Read with a partner: Appointment confirmation

Agent: Thank you for calling. I can confirm your appointment for Thursday, July 16, at 3:00 PM.

Customer: Is that at the downtown office?

Agent: Yes, at our office on Oak Street, in the city center.

Customer: I thought it was on Friday. Could you check the date again?

Agent: Of course. I have Thursday, July 16, at 3:00 PM. Would you like to reschedule?

Customer: No, Thursday works. Can you repeat the address?

Agent: Certainly. It's at 24 Oak Street. Please arrive ten minutes early.

Customer: Got it. Thursday, July 16, at 3:00 PM, at 24 Oak Street. Thank you!

Language guide

AT for exact times and specific points: at 3:00 PM; at the front desk.

ON for days/dates and streets: on Thursday; on July 16; on Oak Street.

IN for months, years, and larger areas: in July; in 2026; in the city center.

BY means no later than a deadline. BETWEEN describes a range.

Expression bank · Flip the cards

Discuss

Why does the customer ask the agent to check again? How does the agent clarify the appointment without guessing?

03 · Remember: Check the Details

12 minutes · Complete all ten questions, then check your answers. Every answer can be corrected and checked again.

04 · Internalize: Schedule Detective

13 minutes · Analyze booking records and confirmation messages. Find the mismatches before practicing customer calls in Fluent Use.

Round 1 · Find and Fix (6 min)

Compare the original booking with the message. Identify which details are wrong. Select the corrected answers and check them.

Round 2 · Explain and Verify (7 min)

With a partner, explain each error in a complete sentence. Rewrite one accurate confirmation, then decide which details must be verified before sending it.

Language support

The booking says Thursday, July 16, but the message says Friday, July 17. The correct date is Thursday, July 16.

Use on for dates, at for exact times and specific venues, and on for streets.

Partner discussion: What should you verify before sending a confirmation?

05 · Fluent Use: Customer-Service Confirmations

15 minutes · Practice both scenarios. Use the timer for each round and reflection.

06:00

Scenario 1 · Confirming an Appointment (6 min)

Agent: Confirm a medical appointment for Wednesday, July 29, at 10:30 AM, at 80 Cedar Road. The customer thinks it is at 11:30 AM. Explain the correct time politely and ask them to repeat the final details.

Customer: You think your appointment is at 11:30 AM. Ask for confirmation and clarify the location.

Scenario 2 · Confirming Package Delivery (6 min)

Agent: Confirm a delivery scheduled for Friday, July 31, between 2:00 and 6:00 PM, at 16 River Lane, Apartment 5. The customer gives Apartment 8. Verify the address and explain that the address must be corrected before delivery.

Customer: Ask when your package will arrive, confirm the delivery window, and correct the apartment number.

Success criteria

State the date, time or time window, and address accurately. Clarify one mismatch. Use polite confirmation questions and a final summary.
